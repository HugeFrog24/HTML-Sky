Fonts bundled with HT's Mod Loader (winhttp.dll)
=================================================

The loader bundles two fonts so its ImGui overlay can render the scripts real
UI text and player nicknames use (Latin, Cyrillic, Georgian, Vietnamese and
CJK). They are compiled into winhttp.dll as resources (src/ui/fonts.rc) and
load at startup.

DejaVuSans.ttf
  DejaVu Sans 2.37. Based on Bitstream Vera; DejaVu changes are in the public
  domain. See LICENSE-DejaVu.txt. http://dejavu-fonts.org/

DroidSansFallback.ttf
